ASIATECHNOSTROY · ENGINEERING GROUP

Engineering operations and maintenance as a managed system

AsiaTechnoStroy organises engineering operations and maintenance for commercial facilities in Tashkent. Engineering systems, scheduled work, documentation and delivery control are brought into one operating framework.

Before maintenance begins, the equipment scope, work boundaries, communication process and reporting format are defined. These parameters become the basis of the facility’s technical operating plan.
